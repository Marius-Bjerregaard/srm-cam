# Review of 2026-11-20 (fork 0.22.1.2, on upstream 0.22.1)

A full read-through of the program and the Arduino firmware, in six areas
(the run and its probing, the machine link and the spoolers, the Run it page
and the main window, the CAM core, the laser, the firmware and its
protocol). Every fix has a test; the whole suite passes (2230). Nothing here
has been run at the mill yet.

## Firmware (`hardware/srm20_spi_probe`) - NOT flashed

New firmware goes on the lab's Arduino only after Mads approves it in a pull
request. Compiled for the Uno (51 % flash). What changed is listed in
`hardware/README.md`, "Firmware v5 safety fixes". The biggest:

- a retract never goes down or sideways (it went to the last datum's height,
  below the head, on STOP);
- the first touch after a datum is a fine one (it stepped 150 um at a time,
  pressing a still bit up to 0.15 mm into the copper);
- a command with missing or garbled numbers is refused (`O` alone set the
  origin to machine 0,0,0; a short `M` moved the bit to the copper's height).

## Fixed in the program

- **STOP**: Page Up after STOP (which the bar suggests) cancelled the STOP
  during a run; a run waiting at the flip or the check ignored STOP; an
  unexpected error mid-job left the job spooling; a silent Arduino read as a
  finished job; STOP during a touch was taken for "no copper here".
- **Into the stock**: the copper search stepped down through a hole into the
  bed; a hole's reference touch landed in the next hole (header pins); the
  hole search walked into no-go zones; the surface grid came down on the
  bracket's tongue; the step-down trusted a single position read.
- **Wrong place**: inch Gerber/Excellon files (holes squashed 25.4x); the
  double-sided export's cut-out cut in the bottom's frame (through an
  L-shaped board); oval holes and the line-up on a double-sided board in
  the wrong frame (8 mm); holes from every drill file in the folder;
  "holes again" at the bottom's check used the top's fit; the board could
  be dragged, undone or swapped while a run was going.
- **Wrong depth**: short gap cuts (and pieces between tabs) spent their
  length ramping and never reached depth; a turned board's height map fell
  back to a plane (a 0.2 mm bow mapped as 0.03); copper arcs were dropped.
- **Wrong machine**: on Linux any Roland device (a vinyl cutter) was the
  mill; Windows sent behind old jobs in the spooler, and ended a half-written
  job.
- **The laser**: STOP did not stop a banded job (the next band was fired); the
  job could change between bands (Mirror mid-run etched band 2 mirrored);
  the field test cut its coupons at the wrong heights; a stale focus map was
  used for another face; after a lost link the head moved while the laser
  may still have been etching.
- Smaller: a second Connect locked the port; settings carried over to the
  next board (Start at, line-up, oval holes, start click); a project from
  another PC rewrote this mill's bracket; `.srmproj` meant two formats;
  Ctrl+3 did nothing; the Mac's Update now rebuilt from upstream, not the
  fork.

## Not changed - for the machine, or for a decision

- **RML start lift** (`backends/srm20.py`, `safe_start`): RML programs start
  with a diagonal move from wherever the head is, right after spindle on.
  A fix was written (commit 503f8a1) and reverted: it changes every RML
  job and was never run on the mill. Try it there in RML mode.
- **Firmware, needs measuring**: `waitForMotorStop` returns "done" when the
  ready pin is low and decides a move is over on one status read; `U`, `M`,
  `N` have no depth limit of their own; the PAUSE bit is out of the guard;
  probing is not refused while a VPanel spindle turns.
- **The run**: a job held at the mill's own Pause for 20 s is taken as
  done (the pause bit's meaning is unproven); the edge walk on a drilled
  sheet looks 2-4 mm past the edge, where a clamp may stand; the hole
  search's edge walks can reach 4 mm past a ring point unchecked against
  no-go zones.
- **CAM**: slots are cut as a row of overlapping plunges (heavy side load on
  the bit); a hole more than twice the bit frees a loose disc; clear
  (negative) Gerber objects count as copper; only the largest Edge.Cuts
  shape is cut out; a panel loses its net names; the two time estimates
  disagree by about 12 %.
- **Laser**: `wait()` may take a not-yet-started job for done; a stencil
  ignores the focus map it says it will use; autofocus and the map
  correct differently outside the built-in reference's area.
- **Link**: Linux usblp's non-blocking writes may not surface a failed
  transfer; usblp allows one open at a time (EBUSY right after a purge);
  some Debian/Ubuntu systems blacklist usblp.
